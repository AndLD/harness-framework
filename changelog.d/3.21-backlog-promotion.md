Changed: a backlog item is a proposal the owner has not approved, and it is never started as it is. Its status is `Proposed` or `Promoted` (with `Promoted to:`), not ⬜, 🟡 or ✅; it has no branch, no claim and no log; and an order of development, an execution order or a `Needs` that names a proposed item is a problem of `check plan`. The owner's decision is `harness plan promote <B-n> --phase <n> [--agent <marker>] [--size <n>] --approved`, which writes the next task of the phase from the item, sets the item to `Promoted`, moves it to the archive and updates the size of the phase. The backlog index lists the proposals, the archive the promoted items with their tasks, and the session start says how many proposals wait. This replaces the statuses and the `feature/B-<n>` branch of the backlog of the first development versions.
