Added: `harness flow check | status <n> | next <n> | prompt <n> <step>`, the tools of the flows of the plan (the procedure `run-flow`). `harness flow check`, also the check `flows` of `harness check all`, holds the parts of a flow file and of its log, unique step ids (an inserted step with a suffix after its base), that the ids a step names exist (tasks and issues, never a backlog item), that a version is a row of the releases file that the flow declares, that the `Needs` of steps point to earlier steps and those of flows resolve without a circle, and that the flows index agrees with the files. `harness flow status` recomputes the state of each step from the repository, not from the State column (the ids of a `tasks` step ✅ with their pull requests merged, the tag of a `cut`, the version in the consumer's lock for a `consumer-take`), prints the mismatches with the column and the log, a step an orchestrator left open, and the next step; `harness flow next` prints that step with its prompt, and `harness flow prompt` the prompt of a step, rendered from the standard wording in the new `core/plan/flow-step-prompts.md` (a `custom` step carries its own text). The session start names a flow that is running or waits, with its next step. A project without flows has nothing to do; one with flows runs `harness flow check`, which `check all` now includes.
