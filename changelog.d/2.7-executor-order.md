Changed: the order of the executors of a flow (a change of the meaning of the procedure `run-flow` and of the agent-runtime contract, approved by the owner on 2026-11-05). The runtime contract (still version 2, a minor addition) gains the executor `cli`, a worker on the runtime's command line, and sets an order: `session` (a session the owner can open in the runtime's application) is the default of every step, `cli` is its fallback for one step at a time, and `manual` the fallback of that; a `subagent` stands outside the order, for a short step of analysis only. `harness flow dispatch` prints, beside every start, the next executor of the order with the log line that names it as the fallback and its reason (`- **Executor:** cli, the fallback of session: <the reason> · …`), and a step in another repository goes to `cli` with its reason (a session cannot be counted on to start in another folder without the owner's message). `harness flow check` refuses a "step started" entry on another executor than the step's own without the fallback and its reason, and the reuse rule never continues a worker of another executor, so the next step goes back to a session. The flow settings and a step's override accept `cli`. The Claude Code preset splits its `session` executor in two: `session` is the desktop app's sessions, and `cli` the command line (`claude -p`, with the flow's `permissions` as flags). A project whose flows use the preset has nothing to do; a log line written before reads as before. A runtime adapter of the project's own that offered the command line as a means of its `session` should move it to a `cli` executor, which is optional.
