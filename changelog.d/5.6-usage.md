Added: usage in the flows. The agent-runtime contract gains the optional role `usage` (`usageFor(profile)`): how an orchestrator reads the account's windows, and a table of coefficients that turns a step's SP, model and kind into a share of the 5-hour and the weekly window (money on an API key). The Claude Code preset reads them from the desktop app (`get_usage`) and carries provisional coefficients for Pro, Max 5x, Max 20x and the API; the Cursor preset asks the owner and has a mock; a runtime that leaves the role out gets the owner asked. New: `harness flow estimate <n>` sizes a flow, `harness flow gate <n> <step> --five-hour <percent>@<reset> --weekly <percent>@<reset>` says whether a step may start (GO, WAIT until a reset, STOP, ASK) with the safety factor on its estimate, and `harness flow sample` appends measurements to the flows' `usage.jsonl`; `harness flow dispatch` prints the estimate, how to read the usage and the gate; `harness flow check` flags a step that cannot fit one window where the subscription is known, and a bad line of `usage.jsonl`. The flow settings `usage` gain `safetyFactor` (1.2) and `maxWaitMinutes` (300), and are merged one level deeper, so a flow that sets only its subscription keeps the default thresholds. A project sets its subscription in `usage.subscription` of the ignored `harness-profile.local.json`; nothing else to do.
