Added: the examples (task 1.4). `examples/profile/` is a filled profile of a fictitious project, `examples/product/` its product specification, and `examples/plan/` a sample plan with one phase and two tasks with their logs; the framework's tests hold each to the check it exercises, and `examples/README.md` explains them. The bootstrap now writes the **product-knowledge skeleton** into a new project's `docs/product/` (an index, a glossary, a register of open questions and a backlog, from `core/templates/scaffold/product-*.md`), sets `productKnowledge` and the document roles `specification` and `glossary` in its profile file, and adds a row for the specification to the reading map of its entry file, so `harness check product-knowledge` runs on every new project. A project that bootstrapped before has no such files and no `productKnowledge`, and nothing changes for it.
