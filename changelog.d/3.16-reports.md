Added: reports from users are taken in and sent upstream by procedures, set by switches in the profile file. `reports.intake` (`off`, `notify`, `auto`) and `reports.triageLimit`: the session start lists the open reports and those not triaged yet, and with `auto` the agent triages at most the limit before other work by the procedure `triage-reports`, which looks for the same or a close problem in the issue queue, its archive and the open tasks before adding a new issue. `reports.upstream` (`off`, `ask`, `post`): the procedure `report-upstream` drafts and posts a suspected defect of the framework. `harness reports …` reaches the host through its adapter's new `reports` role (GitHub: `gh`); answering, closing and creating need the owner's approval. A local, ignored `harness-profile.local.json` overrides the switches at once. A switch that is on adds one line to the entry file's Git rules; one that is off adds nothing, no skill and no row of the reading map.
