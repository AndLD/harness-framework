Changed: a flow may decide a change of the meaning of a contract at a level of its settings (a change of the meaning of the procedure `run-flow`, approved by the owner on 2026-11-06). The flow's `levels` gain the kind `contract.change`, D3 by default: a flow in brainstorm mode decides it below its `askAt` and asks from it up (with the default `askAt` D2 it still asks the owner), and a flow in seamless mode decides it and lists it in the digest to review later. The worker names the change as an open decision of its step report and the orchestrator decides it as any other; the pull request of the change cites the decision entry of the flow's log as the approval, or the record of the step does when the change merged first, and a refusal reverts it. A change of the meaning of a core rule or of a non-negotiable stays the owner's whatever the mode. A project has nothing to do; a flow that should keep asking for every contract change in seamless mode cannot, so such a project runs that flow in brainstorm mode, with `askAt` at D3 or below.
