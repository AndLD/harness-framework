Added: the standard step prompts of `tasks`, `consumer-take` and `triage-fix` steps gain a paragraph (section `delegate` of `core/plan/flow-step-prompts.md`) that tells the worker to hand a large log, a long diff, a long test output or a research of sources to a subagent that returns a summary of at most 10 lines with pointers as evidence, and never to delegate a decision, a write, a merge or a reading a procedure requires in full; the procedure `start-task` and the runtime contract's `subagent` executor say the same. Nothing to do for a project: the prompts are rendered from the framework's copy, and a runtime without a `subagent` executor leaves the reading to the worker.
