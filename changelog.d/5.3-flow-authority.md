Changed: a flow the owner started may cut releases and answer reports at the levels its settings give them (a change of the meaning of core rules, approved by the owner in its pull request). The Git rule `agent.ask-merge` lets the orchestrator of such a flow approve the merges of a release or a hotfix when the cut's level (by default a development version D1, a final release or a hotfix D3) is below the flow's `askAt`, or the flow is seamless; it merges by merge commit once the required checks are green, never by the host's auto-merge or an administrator's bypass, tags, and logs the cut as a decision with its level. The procedure `release` says what it does in the owner's place ("When a flow cuts": it reviews the migration note by the unbiased analysis of `run-flow` first). The report rules and the procedure `triage-reports` let such a flow approve answers and closings on the host at their level (D4 by default); creating a report on the host stays the owner's. Nothing changes for a project without flows. The shell guard still refuses auto-merge on release and hotfix pull requests, and the rule-to-check map shows what holds a flow's cut.
