Changed: the harness review method counts the `procedures` block of the entry file apart (its rows and words, in M6, and its rows in the rule load M20) and the bindings of a project set a ceiling for both; the bindings stub of a new project has a `Budgets` section for them. The cold-start probes of a project include the extension of a document (add a phase to the plan; put a defect found in use into the plan): the stub ships them as P5 and P6 beside the routine ones, and `starting-probes.md` says what to record. The method also says how a probe runs: a probe of another repository, or one that depends on the session start, runs as a separate session started in that repository, because a subagent of the reviewing session inherits that session's entry file and gets no session-start hook. A project that already has bindings adds the two probes and its budget at its next review; nothing else to do.
