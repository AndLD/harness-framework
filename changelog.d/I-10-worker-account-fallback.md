Fixed: a flow continued from another account (an owner with two subscriptions on one machine) no longer resumes a worker that account cannot find. The flow-log template's "step started" line gains an `Account:` field; the runtime contract (still version 2) gains an optional operation of an executor, `account()`, the means to read the account the orchestrator runs on (the Claude Code preset: `printenv CLAUDE_CODE_USER_EMAIL` in the desktop app, `claude auth status` on the command line); `harness flow dispatch` takes `--account <id>`, and the reuse rule adds "the same account" when both are known. Whenever `dispatch` continues a worker, it also prints the fresh start to fall back on when the continue fails for any reason, and the procedure `run-flow` says to take it and log a D0 decision instead of retrying. Nothing to do for a project: a log without `Account:` fields reads as before, and an adapter without `account` is not compared.
