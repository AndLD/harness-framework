Added: templates for a risk register and for design records (RFCs), the two cross-cutting documents the plan method names but the framework shipped nothing for. The bootstrap writes them once into a new project, at the new optional roles `documents.riskRegister` and `documents.rfcs` (the starter values are `docs/development-plan/cross-cutting/risk-register.md` and `.../decisions/README.md`): the register with its rules and its table (likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner, status, date reviewed), and the index of the records with the rules (immutable after acceptance, superseded by a new record, cited by the task) and the template of a record. Both appear in the document map of the profile document. No check reads them. A project that already has such documents, or leaves the roles empty, gets nothing; to take the templates, set the two roles and copy the files from `harness/core/templates/scaffold/`.
