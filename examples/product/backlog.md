# Backlog

Ideas and wishes that no plan holds yet, one section each: what is wanted, and for whom. An item that is planned leaves this file, and one that is
decided against is deleted.

## Backlog

### Note templates

Teams that write the same kind of note every week (meeting notes, reviews) want to start from a template. Wanted by team admins.

### Export of a notebook

An owner who leaves wants all notes of a notebook as files. Wanted by owners.
