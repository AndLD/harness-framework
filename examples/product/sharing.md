# Sharing

A **notebook** belongs to one person, its **owner**, or to a **team**. A person who is not the owner reaches a notebook only through a team or a link.

## Teams

- A **team** is a named group of people. A person can be in many teams.
- A team has **members** and **admins**. An admin adds and removes members; the last admin cannot leave the team.
- Sharing a notebook with a team gives its members one **access level** for that notebook: *reader* or *writer*. A person in two teams that share a notebook has the higher level.

## Links

- The owner of a notebook, or a writer, can make a **link** to a note. Anyone who has the link can read that note, without joining a team.
- A link never gives access to other notes of the notebook, and it can be turned off at any time; after that it shows nothing.
- Whether a link can be set to expire on a date is not decided (Q2).
