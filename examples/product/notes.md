# Notes

A **note** is a piece of text with a title, kept in one **notebook**. Everything a person writes in Orbit Notes is a note.

## Writing a note

- A note has a title of up to 120 characters and a body of formatted text. A note without a title is called "Untitled" until the author names it.
- A note is saved as the author types; there is no save button. Two people editing one note at once see each other's changes within a second.
- A note belongs to exactly one notebook. Moving it to another notebook keeps its **history**.

## History

Every saved change of a note is kept in its **history**: who changed it, and when. Any member who can read the note can read its history, and a member who can write
the note can restore an earlier version, which becomes a new change and does not erase the ones after it.

## Deleting a note

A deleted note is hidden for 30 days and can be restored by any member who can write it. After that it is gone, with its history. Whether the owner of a notebook can
shorten that period is not decided (Q1).
