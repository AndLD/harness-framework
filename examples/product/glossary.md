# Glossary

One row per concept of Orbit Notes. A name (a term or an alias) belongs to one concept only. Use – where a column has nothing to say.

| Term | Code name | Aliases | Definition |
| ---- | --------- | ------- | ---------- |
| **Note** | `Note` | page | A piece of text with a title, kept in one notebook. |
| **Notebook** | `Notebook` | folder | The place that holds notes. It has one owner, or a team. |
| **Owner** | `owner` | – | The person a notebook belongs to; the owner always has full access. |
| **Team** | `Team` | group | A named group of people with members and admins. |
| **Member** | `Member` | – | A person in a team. |
| **Admin** | `admin` | – | A member who adds and removes members. |
| **Access level** | `AccessLevel` | role, permission | What a notebook allows a person: reader or writer. |
| **History** | `NoteVersion` | revisions | Every saved change of a note: who changed it, and when. |
| **Link** | `ShareLink` | public link | An address that lets anyone read one note. |
