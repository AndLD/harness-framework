**The first `check all` after taking `0.3.0` fails on the new releases file and on a generic word of the core, and the notes say nothing of either or of the cost of the gate**: the first take of `0.3.0-dev.1` by a consumer (AndLD/bot-rts, step 1a of flow 1) found three things the notes missed. (1) `check layers` refuses the new releases file (`paths.releases`) until a layer of the consumer's layer map holds it: the migration creates the file and does not place it, and the note's required actions do not name the layer. (2) The ordinary word "base" at `core/plan/README.md:217` fails the consumer's `check core` because its product terms hold "base": the scan of the core with a consumer's own terms is the intended check, and a generic word in the core turns into a failure for each consumer with that term. (3) A take changes `harness/**`, which counts as code in a consumer's gate, so the push gate and the CI run every package's whole suite (api, client and mcp, minutes and a database); the notes do not say so. A fix places the releases file in the layer by the migration, or says in a required action of the note which layer to add and proves it with `check layers`; rewords the line of the core that holds the word (and looks for the other generic words of the core that a consumer's terms are likely to hit, with the consumer's list in hand); and adds one line on the cost of the first gate after a take to the note of the version and to the procedure `update-framework`. Found by the worker of step 1a of flow 1.
