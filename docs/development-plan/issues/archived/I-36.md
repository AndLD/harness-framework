# I-36 — `harness flow status` reads a development take whose draft was closed as not done

**Status:** ✅ · **Agent:** C1 · **Size:** 1 SP · **Area:** checks · **Reported:** —

**`harness flow status` reads a development take whose draft was closed as not done**: since I-28 a take that a later take superseded reads ✅ when a merged pull request names its version; flow 1 took `0.3.0-dev.1` to `0.3.0-dev.3` on drafts (AndLD/bot-rts#297 to #299) that were closed when the take of `0.3.0` (#300) merged, so its steps 1a, 1d and 1g read ⬜ and are listed as mismatches with the log, which says they are done. Found by the orchestrator of flow 3. A fix: a take whose version the consumer's lock has passed is done when a pull request names its version, merged or closed (a closed draft of a development take is the way such a take ends); verified by a test of a superseded take with a closed draft, and by `harness flow status 1` showing no mismatch.
