# I-20 — A flow cannot decide a change of the meaning of a contract at a level: it stops for the owner whatever the mode

**Status:** 🟡 · **Agent:** C2 · **Size:** 1 SP · **Area:** procedures · **Reported:** —

**A flow cannot decide a change of the meaning of a contract at a level**: the procedure `run-flow` gives a flow only two of the things the entry file keeps for the owner, a release or hotfix cut and an answer or a closing of a report, each by the flow's `levels`; everything else stops the flow and asks, whatever the mode. A change of the meaning of a contract is one of them (the entry file of the framework, section 9), although the table of levels already names an adapter contract as D3. In flow 2, step 1a (task 2.11) merged a contract change in seamless mode and the orchestrator then stopped for the owner's approval after the merge. The owner decided (2026-11-06): "I want the changes of contract to be among the askAt D levels not at hard limit". The fix: a decision kind `contract.change` (D3 by default) in the flow's `levels`, decided below `askAt` in brainstorm and always in seamless, with the pull request of the change citing the flow's decision entry as the approval; a change of the meaning of a core rule or of a non-negotiable stays the owner's. Found by the orchestrator of flow 2.
