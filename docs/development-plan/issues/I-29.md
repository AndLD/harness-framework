# I-29 — The usage gate does not know the plan `pro` in lower case

**Status:** ⬜ · **Agent:** C1 · **Size:** 1 SP · **Area:** checks · **Reported:** —

**The usage gate does not know the plan `pro` in lower case**: `harness flow gate … --plan pro` and `… --subscription pro` estimate nothing ("no subscription known"), while `--plan Pro`, the form the runtime's usage reading prints, maps to the Pro coefficients; the usage line says `--plan` is the way to give it but not its form, and `estimate` takes no `--plan` at all, so a flow whose local profile sets no `usage.subscription` cannot be sized. Found by the orchestrator of flow 3 on 2026-11-07. A fix: `--plan` matches a plan or a subscription id without regard to case, `estimate` and `sample` take it as `gate` does, and the message names the forms; verified by tests of each form.
