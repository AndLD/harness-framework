# I-28 — `harness flow status` reads a take that a later take superseded as not done

**Status:** ⬜ · **Agent:** C1 · **Size:** 1 SP · **Area:** checks · **Reported:** —

**`harness flow status` reads a take that a later take superseded as not done**: the status of a `consumer-take` step (`takeTruth`, `tools/lib/flow-status.mjs`) compares the version in the consumer's lock with the step's version exactly, so once the consumer takes a newer version every earlier take reads ⬜ and is listed as a mismatch. Flow 1 shows it for its four takes and flow 2 for the take of `0.4.0-dev.1` (bot-rts is on `0.4.0`), and a flow that `Needs` such a flow is reported as waiting for it ("Waits for flow 2, which is not finished") although its log ends with "stop: finished". Found by the orchestrator of flow 3 on 2026-11-07, which started the flow on a D1 decision. A fix: a take is done when its pull request is merged and the lock names the step's version or a later one (by the order of versions, a final version after its development versions); verified by tests of a superseded take and of a flow that needs a finished flow with one.
