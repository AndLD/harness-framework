# I-25 — The list of notes in `migrations/README.md` is written by hand and stops at `0.2.0`

**Status:** ⬜ · **Agent:** C1 · **Size:** 1 SP · **Area:** bootstrap and sync · **Reported:** —

**The list of notes in `migrations/README.md` is written by hand and stops at `0.2.0`**: its section "The notes" links the notes up to `0.2.0`, while `migrations/` holds the notes of `0.3.0-dev.1` to `0.4.0`; nothing writes the list and no check compares it with the folder, so it drifts with every release. Found by the worker of step 7d of flow 2. A fix: the list is complete again, and either `harness release` adds the note of the version it cuts, or a check (in `docs:check`) fails when a note of the folder is missing from the list; verified by a test that plants a note without its line.
