# B-10 — A record of agent sessions that the agent cannot write, and managed limits

**Status:** Proposed · **Size:** 3 SP · **Area:** runtime adapters · **Source:** agent

The [first security review](../../quality-reviews/security/2026-11-08.md) (finding S-20261108-13, low) found that sessions leave no record the agent cannot write, and have no managed limits, so after an incident what a session did rests on its own account. What is wanted: a decision on what to record and where, and a scan of the transcripts, by hand at first.

Filed on 2026-11-08 when Phase 2 was closed, on the owner's word that every finding has a place. It waits for the owner to promote it.
