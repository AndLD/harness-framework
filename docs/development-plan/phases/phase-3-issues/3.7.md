# 3.7 — A pinned project does not scan its copy of `core/` with its own terms

**Status:** ✅ · **Agent:** C2 · **Size:** 3 SP · **Log:** [3.7.log.md](./3.7.log.md)

**A pinned project does not scan its copy of `core/` with its own terms**: in pinned mode `check core` scans nothing, since the framework scans `core/` upstream (`frameworkCoreFolder`). The framework cannot know a consuming project's product words, so one of them can reach a release unseen. In the project the framework was extracted from, `docs:check` scans 0 CORE files. (1) In pinned mode `check core` scans the vendored `core/` with the project's `terms` and the adapters' terms; `check vendored` already proves the files are the tag's. (2) The framework publishes the words its core uses on purpose (for example task, phase, plan, release, review, session, agent) in one list, and the scan never reports a word of that list, whatever a consumer's terms say. (3) A hit names the file, the line and the word, and the two ways out: add an ordinary word to `terms.productGeneric`; report a product or tool word upstream and stay on the current version. Verified by a pinned fixture whose profile lists a word that its vendored `core/` contains: `check core` fails with that message; the same fixture passes with the word in `productGeneric`, and passes when the word is in the framework's vocabulary; and the consuming project of the extraction passes as it is today. Found by the closing review of the extraction (H-20261103-01).
