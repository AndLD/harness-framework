# 5.3 — The authority of a flow: release cuts and answers to reports at their levels

**Status:** ⬜ · **Agent:** C3 · **Size:** 3 SP · **Needs:** 5.2

**The authority of a flow: release cuts and answers to reports at their levels**: today the Git rules of the entry file (section 7.5, `agent.ask-merge`) and step 4 of the procedure `release` let only the owner approve the merge of a release or a hotfix, and the triage rule of section 7.5 and the procedure `triage-reports` let only the owner approve an answer or a closing on the host. The owner decided on 2026-11-04 that an orchestrator may cut releases and answer reports itself, at the levels its flow's settings give them (5.2). Change the rules, which changes the meaning of core rules (the owner's approval in the pull request): (1) a release or hotfix merge, and an answer or a closing on the host, need the owner's approval or that of a flow the owner started, when its settings let it decide at that level; (2) the procedure `release` says what the orchestrator does in the owner's place: it reviews the migration note by the unbiased analysis of `run-flow`, merges the release pull requests by merge commit once the required checks are green (never by the host's auto-merge, never by admin bypass), tags, and logs the cut as a decision with its level; (3) the rule-to-check map. The shell guard keeps refusing auto-merge on release and hotfix pull requests, and nothing lets a flow cross the hard limits of 5.2. Verified by the guard's tests (auto-merge on a release pull request still refused), `npm run docs:check`, and a probe on a scratch project: an orchestrator in brainstorm with the default levels cuts a development version and asks before the final cut.
