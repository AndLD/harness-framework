### 2026-11-06 — the order of the executors, with the command line as an executor of its own
- **Branch:** `feature/2.7-nested-worker-sessions` — done, built by the worker of step 1 of flow 2, which ran on the command line (`claude -p`): the desktop app refused the orchestrator a session start on a turn that a background event began (2026-11-06 00:41), the case this task names.
- **What was built:** (1) The runtime contract gains the executor `cli` (a worker on the runtime's command line) and **the order of the executors**: `session` the default, `cli` its fallback for one step at a time, `manual` the fallback of that, a `subagent` outside the order; a step in another repository goes to `cli` with its reason. A minor addition to contract 2: an adapter without `cli` still conforms. (2) `harness flow dispatch` picks by the order (`pickExecutor`, `nextInOrder`, `pickStepExecutor`), prints beside every start the next executor for this step only, with its start, its permissions, its report, the means to read its own account and the log line that names it as the fallback (`cli, the fallback of session: <the reason>`); a step in another repository is dispatched on `cli` with `EXTERNAL_REASON`, unless the step names its own executor. (3) `harness flow check` refuses a "step started" entry on another executor than the step's own (its override, else the flow's setting, else `session`) without the fallback and its reason; the grammar of the field is in `flow-steps.mjs` (`readExecutorField`, `executorField`), and the entry readers moved to `flow-check.mjs` so the check and the dispatch share them. (4) The reuse rule already refused a worker of another executor, so a `cli` worker is never carried to the next `session` step; a test holds it. (5) The Claude Code preset splits `session` (the desktop app) from `cli` (`claude -p`, with the flow's `permissions` as flags, `claude auth status` for its account, no wake-ups after its run). (6) The procedure `run-flow` (step 4, "Other repositories"), the plan method (section 10), the flow and flow-log templates, the design (section 4.2 and section 12), the new-adapter guide, the skeleton and a changelog fragment.
- **Decisions / deviations from the plan:** the owner's approval of the change of meaning is the entry "2026-11-05 22:40 — decision D3" of the log of flow 2, cited in the pull request. The executor is named `cli` (a short identifier for the settings and the log); the documents call it "a worker on the command line". A flow's setting or a step's override may name `cli` as its own executor (the owner's setting), and then no reason is needed. The older lines of flow 1's log, which wrote the command line under `session`, read as before and pass the check. The permissions of a nested session and the repeated experiment are left to task 2.11, as the split says; the session executor's permission line says only that a session runs under the app's own settings.
- **Problems / blockers / findings:** the worker's own permissions (the flow's `allow` list) refused `python3`, `grep` in a compound line and heredocs with `${…}`; the work went round them with the edit tools and one-off Node scripts in the scratchpad. Whether a `claude -p` worker is woken by a background command after its turn is not verified, so the preset's `cli` says `wakeups: false`.
