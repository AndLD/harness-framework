# 2.11 — A nested worker session asks no more than a worker on the command line under the same settings

**Status:** ✅ · **Agent:** C3 · **Size:** 2 SP

Split from task [2.7](./2.7.md) on 2026-11-05 by the orchestrator of flow 2 (a D2 decision in its log: the 5 SP step of Opus could not fit an empty 5-hour window of the subscription by the provisional coefficients). It builds on 2.7, which sets the order of the executors; the background, the flow 1 history and the experiments of 2026-11-05 are in that task.

**What is wanted:** the permission mode a worker session starts in (the app's `permission_mode`) is decided by the flow's settings, printed by `harness flow dispatch` and logged; what the flow's `permissions` allow is also given to a nested session by the means the runtime has (its mode and the project's allow rules), so a worker asks no more than a `claude -p` worker under the same settings. The experiments of 2026-11-05 used read-only workers; this task repeats them once with a worker that runs `git`, `gh`, `npm` and `node`, to see what it asks in the mode the flow sets, and records what it finds in the task's log.

**Verified by:** tests of the dispatch (the permission mode of a nested session is printed from the flow's settings, and the allow rules it is given match the flow's `permissions`); the conformance suite of the runtime adapter if its contract changes; and the experiment, repeated once and logged.
