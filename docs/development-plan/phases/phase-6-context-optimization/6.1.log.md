### 2026-11-07 — `harness flow audit` and the baseline of flow 1
- **Branch:** `feature/6.1-flow-audit` — done.
- **What was built:** (1) the runtime role `transcripts` (a minor addition to contract 2, optional, logic): `transcriptsFor() → { means, locate({ ids, roots? }), parse(text) }`, in the manifest schema, `core/contracts/runtime.md` and the conformance suite (a check that skips for a runtime without it). The Claude Code adapter fills it in `transcripts.mjs`: `locate` finds a session of the desktop app through its session file (`cliSessionId`) and a worker of the command line by the name of its worktree; `parse` turns a JSONL transcript into neutral `turns` (one per answer, the lines of one message id counted once, with its usage) and `results` (the characters of each tool result). (2) `tools/lib/flow-audit.mjs` (neutral): the workers from the "step started" entries of the flow's log, the orchestrator from `--orchestrator`, the counts by tool, by shell command (the first words; `harness <command> <subcommand>`), by step and by session, the largest results, what entered apart from the peak context and the cache reads, and a `--json` form. (3) `harness flow audit <n> [--orchestrator <id>[,<id>]] [--top <n>] [--json]`. (4) The baseline entry of flow 1 in the phase log.
- **Verified by:** `flow-audit.test.mjs` (a fixture of known sizes counted by hand; a transcript with no usage; an unknown tool; the log's workers; the command on a stub runtime and the refusals), `transcripts.test.mjs` (the parser on a fixture transcript, `locate` on a fixture tree), the conformance test with the role broken and removed; the real run on flow 1 and the hand count of step 1f.
- **Decisions / deviations from the plan:** (a) **Tokens of a result are not rounded per result** (the sum of rounded results drifted from the hand count). (b) **The orchestrator is named by the caller** (`--orchestrator`): the flow log does not record its session id. A later task may make the "running" entry name it. (c) **Per-step attribution is by session**, a worker is the step its first "step started" entry names; the orchestrator's own turns are one row, not split by step (the task says `calibrate` may take it later). (d) No migration note or consumer fixture in this task: the tool and the role are in `tools/` and `adapters/`, which `framework.json` already distributes whole, and the note of `0.5.0` is written at the cut (6.8 holds the consumer fixture of the phase); the changelog fragment is in.
- **Problems / blockers / findings:** the log of flow 1 names its later workers as "`claude -p` named `flow-1-step-1d`" and not by id, so the reader takes names too; `ls`-style keys such as `for f` (a loop) are weak: the key of a shell loop could name its body later.
