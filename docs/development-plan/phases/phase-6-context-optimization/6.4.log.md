### 2026-11-07 — output budgets and the step-report schema
- **Branch:** `feature/6.4-output-budgets` — done.
- **What was built:** (1) method section 8, "Output budgets and short reports" (filtered reads, quiet variants, short closing text with long text in a file of the plan, the report schema and cap), and a line in the definition of done (section 4); (2) the step prompts: a `budget` section that every step gets, and a `report` section with the schema in this order (`Status`, `Merged`, `Decisions`, `Deviations`, `Next step`, then `Recommendations`, last) and the cap; (3) `tools/lib/step-report.mjs`: the cap (`REPORT_CAP`: 30 lines, 2,400 characters, held once and filled into the prompt as `{reportLines}` and `{reportCharacters}`), `checkStepReport` (labels present and in order, the status one of done, partial, blocked, the recommendations last, the cap) and `harness flow report-check <file>`; (4) the procedures `run-flow` (a report over the cap or out of its form is a deviation; the orchestrator's own entries stay short), `start-task`, `open-pull-request` and `end-session` cite the budgets; their packaged skills were regenerated by `harness sync --local`.
- **Measured:** the prompt of a step grows by about 120 words (the `budget` section and the longer `report` section; step 5 of flow 3 renders in 2.9k characters). The cap is chosen from the baseline of 6.1 (the agents' own output was 59% of what entered the context); the fall of the reports' length is not measured here. The dry run of a step with the new prompts under the audit of 6.1 was **not** done: the audit reads the transcripts of a finished flow, and the first steps with these prompts are the next ones; 6.8 measures the fall against the baseline.
- **Decisions / deviations from the plan:** (a) **The wording of the schema is in the step prompts, the cap in the tool** (one place each, with a test that the template names every label in the order the tool checks); the method names both. (b) A heading line `## Recommendations` cannot be in the template (the tool splits it by `## `), so the prompt says "a line of its own", and the check accepts a line `Recommendations`, with or without `#`, bold or a colon. (c) The check is a command, not part of `harness check all`: a report is not a file of the repository. (d) Changing the meaning of procedures and of the definition of done in the method is a `contract.change` of flow 3 (named in the step report); no core rule or non-negotiable changed.
- **Problems / blockers / findings:** none. The worktree has no `node_modules`; Prettier was run from the main checkout's binary.
- **Next step:** 6.5, a restart threshold for the orchestrator, and steps paced for the cache.
