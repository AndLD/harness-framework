# 6.7 — Agent briefs and section reads of the documents, decided on the audit's numbers

**Status:** ⬜ · **Agent:** C3 · **Size:** 2 SP · **Needs:** 6.1

**The documents were not the main sink, so whether to build briefs is a decision, not a plan.** After the baseline of 6.1, this task takes the files that the agents actually read (frequency times size, from the audits of real flows) and decides, with the numbers in the phase log, whether the saving justifies the cost. If it does, it records the design as a task of this phase (so the work is claimed and sized as any other): a brief is **one-way and generated** from the document that stays the source (marked blocks, rationale dropped, rule ids and anchors kept), a check that a brief is current and has every rule id of its source and fits its token budget, a link to the full text at its end, and section-addressed reads by the documents' stable anchors and rule ids (`harness docs get <id>`); it is distributed and rendered into the project's packaged skills like the other generated files. Encoding the documents into abbreviated words was considered and rejected (the model reads it less reliably, and diffs, search and the link check break). If the numbers do not justify it, the entry says so and the task is closed with them.

**For consumers too:** whatever is decided applies to the consumer's documents as well (the generator works on the project's own documents under its own bindings).

**Verified by:** the entry in the phase log with the numbers (the files read, how often, their size, the estimated saving), the decision, and, when it is "build", the new task with its size; `harness check all` passes.
